# paRAnoia

A psychedelic psychological horror(ish) game developed by Psychedelic Camel Studios for IMGD 4000 and 4500 at WPI (B-Term 2026).

## About the Game
After taking an experimental hallucinogen, Goop 53, on their night off, a college Resident Advisor (RA) is unexpectedly called to fill in to complete the nightly rounds. As the drug takes effect, the dorm building becomes surreal and ever-changing, where the line between reality and hallucination becomes indistinguishable. To make it through the night, the RA must navigate distorted hallways, solve mind-bending puzzles, and address bizarre student problems.
